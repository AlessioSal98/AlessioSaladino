  • Sep 2021 – Oct 2024

    Master's Degree in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics

    Sapienza University of Rome · Grade 104/110

    Thesis: A Novel Architecture Integrating LLM and Context-Aware Supervisors for Safe Interaction with Social Robots

    About the thesis

    A framework for safely integrating LLMs with a social robot, overcoming limitations such as hallucinations and bias and preventing ethical and security risks. The architecture leverages long-term memory through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and LLM-based Supervisors manage the interaction flow, so that the robot can communicate fluently, remember past context and user information, and detect and handle dangerous situations. This work was the starting point of my PhD research.
