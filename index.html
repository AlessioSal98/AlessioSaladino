Alessio Saladino

PhD Student in Artificial Intelligence
DIAG — Sapienza University of Rome

I am a PhD student in the 40th cycle of the National PhD in Artificial Intelligence at Sapienza University of Rome, supervised by Prof. Luca Iocchi and in collaboration with Procter & Gamble. My research focuses on integrating Large Language Models with social and industrial robots, developing cognitive architectures that let robots understand their context and act in a safe, coherent and socially appropriate way.

My interest in Human-Robot Interaction started during my bachelor's degree, where I wrote my thesis on social robots for e-health, and grew during my master's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.
